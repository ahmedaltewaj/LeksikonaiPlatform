## Executive Summary

This campaign defines how Leksikon.ai will acquire its first 10+ early users (Phase 1 validation per METRICS.md) from the Danish SME market. The strategy prioritizes high-trust, low-friction channels appropriate for Danish business culture, with an emphasis on word-of-mouth and professional network leverage.

**Target**: 10+ registered Danish SME users within 4 weeks of campaign launch  
**Primary Metric**: Early users registered  
**Secondary Metric**: DAU (5+), Inquiry volume per user per week (10+)  
**Budget**: TBD — no external spend without board approval

## 3. Outreach Channels

### Channel Strategy (Ranked by Trust Level)

| Rank | Channel | Trust Level | Effort | Expected Conversion |
|------|---------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| 1 | Personal network warm outreach | ★★★★★ | High | High |
| 2 | Industry association membership lists | ★★★★ | Medium | Medium |
| 3 | LinkedIn direct outreach | ★★★ | Medium | Medium |
| 4 | Referral program (existing contacts) | ★★★★★ | Low | High |
| 5 | Online community participation | ★★★ | Medium | Medium-Low |

### Channel 1: Personal Network Warm Outreach (Priority)

**Rationale**: Danish SMEs trust personal recommendations over cold marketing. The founding team's network is the highest-value early user source.

**Tactics**:
- Identify 20–30 warm contacts in target SME segments
- Personalized outreach via email/LinkedIn
- Reference shared connections or industry knowledge
- Offer 30-min discovery call before commitment

**Message framework** (see Section 5)

### Channel 2: Industry Association Lists

**Rationale**: Accounting firms (Dansk Erhverv, FSR), real estate (Danish Real Estate Association)

**Tactics**:
- Identify association member directories (public or paid access)
- Extract contact information for decision-makers
- Segment by company size (target: 5–30 employees)
- Personalized cold outreach with association affiliation reference

**Compliance**: Verify GDPR compliance for any purchased/leased contact lists

### Channel 3: LinkedIn Direct Outreach

**Rationale**: Decision-makers are active on LinkedIn; professional context adds legitimacy

**Tactics**:
- Target: "Owners", "Partners", "Managing Directors" at Danish SMEs
- Industry filters: Accounting, Legal Services, Real Estate
- Location filter: Denmark (prioritize Copenhagen, Aarhus, Øresund)
- Connection request with personalized note (not InMail — higher response rate)
- Follow up once after 5 business days

### Channel 4: Referral Program

**Rationale**: Incentivize early users to refer peers — word-of-mouth multiplier

**Tactics**:
- Offer 1 month free extension for each successful referral
- Referral门槛: Referred company must complete onboarding (connect email/website)
- Track referrals via unique invite codes
- Thank/reward publicly (with permission) as early adopter champion

### Channel 5: Online Community Participation

**Rationale**: Presence in relevant communities builds brand awareness and credibility

**Tactics**:
- Identify: Dansk Erhverv forums, Reddit r/Denmark business threads, relevant Facebook groups
- Participate genuinely (not spam): answer questions, share insights
- Soft mention of Leksikon.ai when relevant to conversation
- Do NOT post promotional content in communities

## 5. Messaging Framework

### Core Message

**Problem**: Danish SME owners spend hours daily on customer email responses — time that could go to revenue-generating work.

**Solution**: Leksikon.ai handles the first draft of customer responses using AI trained on Danish business communication. You review, approve, and send in seconds.

**Proof point**: "Save 2+ hours per week on customer inquiries" (to be validated with early user data)

### Persona-Specific Messaging

#### Accounting Firm Partner

**Subject**: [Name], kan AI hjælpe dine kunder med fakturaspørgsmål?

**Body outline**:
- Hook: "I know you're managing [X] client emails daily about invoice status..."
- Problem: "That's [Y] hours/week that could go to billable work"
- Solution: "Leksikon.ai drafts responses to common client inquiries. You review and send."
- Credibility: "Built for Danish business communication, OIOUBL-compliant"
- CTA: "Would you be open to a 20-minute demo? Free for the first 3 months."

#### Real Estate Agency Ops Lead

**Subject**: [Name], sværger du også på hurtige svar på ejendomsforespørgsler?

**Body outline**:
- Hook: "Property24数据显示:快速回复的客户咨询转化率高..."
- Problem: "Every hour you wait is a potential client choosing a competitor"
- Solution: "Leksikon.ai drafts responses to property inquiries in seconds. Danish language included."
- Credibility: "Used by Danish real estate agencies to stay competitive"
- CTA: "Try it free for 3 months. No commitment."

#### Legal Practice Office Manager

**Subject**: [Name], hvordan håndterer du lige nu kundekorrespondance?

**Body outline**:
- Hook: "Client correspondence is [X]% of your day..."
- Problem: "Every draft takes time from billable legal work"
- Solution: "Leksikon.ai drafts responses. You maintain control — approve every word before it goes."
- Credibility: "Confidentiality is built in — your data, your rules"
- CTA: "Would you evaluate it for your practice? Free pilot, no commitment."

### Tone Guidelines

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Professional but approachable | Salesy or pushy |
| Danish language (always) | English-only outreach |
| Acknowledge their time is valuable | Long paragraphs — keep it scannable |
| Be specific about value | Vague promises |
| Ask for 20-min call, not "sign up now" | Demand immediate commitment |
| Reference Danish business norms | Generic global marketing copy |
