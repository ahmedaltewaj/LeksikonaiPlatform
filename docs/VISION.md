# Leksikon.ai Vision

## Vision Statement

Build and launch a profitable AI-powered application that delivers measurable ROI to Danish SMEs, establishing Leksikon.ai as the trusted AI partner for Denmark's small business community.

## Mission

Leksikon.ai achieves this by conducting thorough market research to identify the highest-value pain points, building AI-powered solutions that address those pain points with Danish language support and local business process integration, and delivering clear ROI demonstration that justifies SME investment.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Timeframe |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Product-Market Fit | 50+ paying Danish SME customers | 6 months |
| Monthly Revenue | €10,000 MRR | 12 months |
| Customer Satisfaction | NPS 40+ | 12 months |
| Retention | 80% month-3 retention | 6 months |
| Danish Market Presence | Top-5 Danish AI solution for SMEs | 18 months |

## Strategic Milestones

1. **Market Research Complete** (Month 1-2): Comprehensive Danish SME market analysis with competitor positioning and identified top 3 pain points
2. **MVP Launch** (Month 3): Working product deployed with core feature solving the #1 pain point, accessible to early adopters
3. **Product-Market Fit** (Month 6): 50+ customers using the product, measurable ROI demonstrated, feedback incorporated into iteration
4. **Revenue Generation** (Month 9): Sustainable pricing model generating €5K MRR with clear expansion path
5. **Scale Ready** (Month 12-18): Profitable operation with €10K MRR, documented Nordic expansion opportunity

## Non-Goals

- **Not** building consumer AI products — we focus exclusively on B2B SME solutions
- **Not** pursuing enterprise clients initially — SME accessibility and affordability come first
- **Not** expanding beyond Nordic markets without proven Danish success
- **Not** racing to be the cheapest — we compete on value and ROI, not price
- **Not** building everything at once — one core feature, validated before expanding
